02Set the rules

You choose what your AI chatbot answers

Nora, Kong's AI chatbot, only answers the kinds of guest questions you've allowed. One tour operator told us their last chatbot kept getting them into trouble by telling guests the wrong inclusions.

  • FAQsPricesAvailability

    What Nora answers

    You pick the kinds of guest questions Nora may answer, such as FAQs, prices and availability. Nora doesn't answer anything you haven't switched on.

    What Nora answers

  • What happens when Nora can't answer

    When a guest asks something Nora isn't allowed to answer, you decide what happens next:

    • Nora passes the chat to your team, so a person joins the conversation.
    • Nora gives the guest your WhatsApp, phone and email, so they can reach you directly.
    What happens when Nora can't answer

  • Or use live chat without AI

    Switch Nora off and the chat on your website works as plain live chat. Every guest message comes to you and your team.
