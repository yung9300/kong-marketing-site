The State of Tour Operator Bookability in Southeast Asia › Indonesia

Kong Research · Edition 2026

What % of tour and activity operators in Indonesia are bookable online?

Of 456 Indonesian tour and activity operators, 37 let customers book directly on their website, 8.1%. Sampled across 8 destinations.

The finding

64.1% of operators with a website take bookings on WhatsApp

Among Indonesian operators with a website but no checkout, 64.1% take bookings through WhatsApp, the highest rate in the region by twenty points. Indonesia has not skipped booking technology; it just adopted a different one.

Bali, the most visited destination in the sample, is only 6.2% bookable. Labuan Bajo, a fraction of its size, manages 16.1%, and Nusa Penida 15.0%. The Komodo liveaboard trade sells online; the Bali day-tour trade does not.

Cooking classes, batik and other cultural workshops make up 16.9% of the Indonesian market, and only 2.6% of them are bookable, the lowest category figure anywhere in this study.

Questions

Common questions

What share of Indonesian tour operators can be booked online?

8.1%: 37 of 456 classified Indonesian tour and activity operators let customers book directly on their website. A further 274 have a website that cannot take a booking, 41 have only a social media profile, and 104 have no web presence at all.

What booking software do Indonesian tour operators use?

WooCommerce on 33, Bokun on 2, Checkfront on 1, GetYourGuide on 1. Dedicated booking platforms reach 4 operators, 0.9% of the Indonesian sample. Most bookable operators use general shopping-cart software rather than proper reservation software.

Do Indonesian tour operators accept local payment methods?

6 of 37 bookable Indonesian operators accept a local payment method such as QRIS or GoPay. The remainder are card-only, which in practice means selling to international travellers rather than domestic ones.

How many Indonesian tour operators have no website?

104 of 456, or 22.8%. Online presence (a website or a social profile) reaches 77.2% of Indonesian operators.

How do Indonesian tour operators take bookings without a checkout?

Mostly by message. Of Indonesian operators with a working website but no online checkout, 64.1% list WhatsApp as their booking channel. Across the country 165 operators use WhatsApp as their only route to a booking.
