How it works

What is this, in plain words?

ChatGPT and Claude can now connect to the apps you already use. Kong's connector lets your assistant look things up in your Kong account and make changes when you ask. It runs on MCP (Model Context Protocol), an open standard that ChatGPT and Claude both support. Kong is used by tour and activity operators in Malaysia, Australia, Thailand and Vietnam, and the connector works wherever you are.

Restech 2.0

Restech 2.0 is when your booking software gets a co-operator: an AI that runs the day with you, inside your rules. Nobody sells you one. You assemble it from a brain like Claude or ChatGPT and the tools it can talk to. This connector is Kong's part: the hands.

Three jobs that cross apps

A guest enquiry, start to finish

  1. GmailA guest emails: "Do you have space Saturday morning for 4?"
  2. KongChecks Saturday. The 9am Rainforest ride has room for 4.
  3. GmailEmails the guest back with the times that have room.
  4. GmailThe guest replies: "9am works, book us in."
  5. KongBooks them in and sends the guest their confirmation.

Questions answered from your own SOPs

  1. GmailA guest asks: "Can my 10-year-old ride, and is there hotel pickup?"
  2. Google DriveReads your SOP for the age rule and the pickup details.
  3. GmailAnswers the guest, with the times that have room.
  4. GmailThe guest confirms by email.
  5. KongMakes the booking and sends the confirmation.

Partner bookings from a shared calendar

  1. Google CalendarA hotel partner writes a booking into the calendar you share with them.
  2. KongAdds it as a booking and credits the partner for the sale.
  3. Google CalendarMarks the event as confirmed so the partner can see it went in.

To run these, connect Gmail, Google Drive or Google Calendar to your assistant the same way you connect Kong. Each app has its own connector in Claude and ChatGPT; Kong only handles the booking side.

You stay in control

Will the AI change bookings without asking me?

No. Here's exactly where the line sits.

Asks you first

  • Taking a booking (the guest gets notified)
  • Changing a price or making a discount code
  • Recording a payment
  • Assigning guides or ATVs to a ride
  • Blocking out one ATV, boat or staff member

Kong blocks until you say yes

  • A cancellation with a fee (Kong quotes the fee first)
  • Overbooking a full ride (your yes is recorded against your name)

Stays in the Kong app

  • Refunds through your card processor
  • New booking questions and gift cards

Data: guest data stays in Kong. Only a guest's email address, used to contact them, and their booking activity, date and time leave Kong. Guest names and contact details stay off your Google Calendar unless you switch them on.

Not yet: website changes and inviting your team can't be done through the connector.

FAQ

Questions operators ask

Which tour booking software works with ChatGPT and Claude?

Kong works with both. Connect your Kong account to ChatGPT or Claude once, then ask your assistant to check availability, take or move bookings, or show your sales. The table above shows which other booking software connects, checked 29 September 2026.

How do I connect Kong to Claude?

In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector. Name it Kong, paste https://api.bookwithkong.com/mcp and click Add. Sign in with your normal Kong login and click Allow. Then ask your first question, such as "What's on tomorrow?". It works on any Claude plan and costs nothing.

How do I connect Kong to ChatGPT?

The steps are the same as for Claude: add Kong as a connector in ChatGPT with the address https://api.bookwithkong.com/mcp, sign in with your normal Kong login and click Allow, then ask your first question. Pasting that address is the only thing to fill in. It works on any ChatGPT plan and costs nothing.

What can ChatGPT or Claude do in my Kong account?

It can search and look up bookings, take bookings including walk-ins, reschedule, cancel, approve and check guests in, resend confirmations, record payments and check waiver status. It can also edit tours, change prices, close days, log expenses, make discount codes and show your sales and profit and loss. Refunds, new booking questions and gift cards stay in the Kong app. Website changes and inviting your team can't be done through the connector yet.

Will the AI change bookings or prices without asking me?

No. Kong tells your assistant to show you exactly what it will change and wait for your yes before it takes a booking, changes a price, makes a discount code or records a payment. Kong itself blocks a cancellation with a fee and any overbooking of a full ride until you confirm, and your yes is recorded against your name.

Can the AI see my guests' card details?

No. Card details are never stored in Kong. They stay with your payment processor, and Kong cannot pass them on, so ChatGPT and Claude cannot see them.

Does it cost extra?

No. Connecting Kong to ChatGPT or Claude is free, and it works on any Claude or ChatGPT plan. Your Kong plan doesn't change.

Do I need to be technical?

No. There's no code to write. You add Kong as a connector with one address, https://api.bookwithkong.com/mcp, sign in with your normal Kong login and click Allow. If you can log in to Kong, you can connect it.

Can my staff connect their own assistant?

Yes. Each team member connects with their own Kong login, and their assistant can only do what that person's Kong role allows.

Does it work in Bahasa Malaysia?

Yes. Ask in Bahasa Malaysia or English and your assistant answers in the language you used. Your Kong data stays exactly as you entered it.

What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude connect to other apps. Kong's connector runs on it. You don't need to know how it works: connect Kong once, and your assistant can look things up and make changes in your account when you ask.

What is restech?

Restech is the industry's word for reservation technology: the booking software tour and activity operators run on, like Kong, Rezdy or FareHarbor. You assemble it from a brain like Claude or ChatGPT and the tools it can talk to. Kong's connector is the hands.
