4. Fees and Payment Terms

4.1 Operator Fees

No Subscription Fees: Kong does not charge monthly or annual subscription fees to operators. The Platform is provided free of charge for core functionality.

4.2 Booking Fees

Guest-Paid Fees: Kong charges a Booking Fee to guests at the time of booking. This fee is currently set at 1.8% of the booking value but may be adjusted with reasonable notice.

Fee Structure: The Booking Fee is:

  • Charged to and paid by the guest
  • Added to the booking total at checkout
  • Clearly disclosed to guests before payment
  • Non-refundable on guest-initiated cancellations — booking fees are refunded when the operator cancels, when a Kong platform error caused the booking, or when Kong has declared an emergency (see Refund Policy)
  • Subject to change with at least 30 days' notice to operators

4.3 Payment Processing

Direct Payment Gateway Integration: You must connect your own payment gateway account (such as Stripe, Xendit, or other supported providers) to the Platform.

When a guest makes a booking:

  • The booking amount is processed directly through your connected payment gateway account
  • Proceeds are deposited directly into your payment gateway account
  • The Booking Fee (1.8%) is automatically split and transferred to Kong's account
  • You maintain full control and visibility over your payment processing

Your Responsibilities: By connecting a payment gateway, you agree to:

  • Maintain an active account in good standing with your chosen payment gateway provider
  • Comply with your payment gateway provider's terms and conditions
  • Accept responsibility for all payment gateway fees charged by your provider
  • Ensure your payment gateway account details remain current and accurate
  • Handle any payment gateway account issues directly with your provider

Payment Gateway Requirements: You must use a payment gateway that:

  • Is supported by the Kong Platform
  • Supports split payment functionality for Booking Fee collection
  • Complies with applicable payment card industry standards (PCI-DSS)
  • Operates legally in your jurisdiction

4.4 Payment Settlement

Direct Settlement: Booking proceeds are settled directly to your payment gateway account according to your payment gateway provider's settlement schedule. Kong does not hold, process, or transfer booking funds to you.

Settlement Timing: Settlement timing is determined by:

  • Your payment gateway provider's policies and schedule
  • Your payment gateway account status and history
  • Payment method used by the guest (credit card, e-wallet, etc.)
  • Any holds or reserves imposed by your payment gateway provider

First Payments: Initial settlements to new payment gateway accounts may be subject to longer holding periods as determined by your payment gateway provider for fraud prevention and verification purposes.

Currency: Settlements are made in the currencies supported by your payment gateway account and configured in your Kong Platform settings.

4.5 Refunds and Chargebacks

Refund Processing: You are responsible for processing refunds through your payment gateway account according to your cancellation policy. When you issue a refund:

  • You refund the booking amount to the guest through your payment gateway
  • The Booking Fee (1.8%) is non-refundable to the guest
  • Guests should be informed that booking fees are non-refundable per your cancellation policy
  • Payment processing fees charged by your payment gateway provider may also be non-refundable (per your provider's policies)

Non-Refundable Booking Fees: Kong's Booking Fee is charged for the service of processing the booking and is non-refundable on guest-initiated cancellations. Booking fees are refunded when the operator cancels, when a Kong platform error caused the booking, or when Kong has declared an emergency — see the Refund Policy for the full schedule. When a guest-initiated cancellation applies, the Booking Fee compensates Kong for:

  • Platform availability and infrastructure
  • Payment processing facilitation
  • Booking confirmation and communication services
  • Administrative overhead

Chargebacks: In the event of a chargeback:

  • Kong is not responsible for chargeback disputes or associated fees
