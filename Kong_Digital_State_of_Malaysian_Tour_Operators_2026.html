Only 8% of Malaysian tour operators can take an online booking.

We hand-audited 252 tour, activity and experience operators across Malaysia, one by one. Only 8% can take a booking online on a channel they own. 74% have no website of their own at all. The rest use WhatsApp, Instagram, walk-ins, or someone else's platform. By Blake Ng, co-founder of Kong · Published 1 July 2026 · Sample n = 252.

Key statistics (Kong, 2026)

8%
Only 8% of Malaysian tour and activity operators can take an online booking on a channel they own (n=252).
74%
74% have no website of their own — reachable only via WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, a Google Business Profile, or a marketplace (n=252).
70%
Among operators with a live website, 70% still cannot take a booking on it — the site takes enquiries only (n=66).
30%

Methodology

Census, not survey. A sample of 252 independent operators was built by searching Google Maps across 14 Malaysian regions and 14 activity types. Website verification: each operator was checked by hand for a website of their own, with indexation confirmed against Semrush domain-rank data; social, messaging or marketplace presence alone counts as offline. Booking-mechanism review: all 66 operators with a working website were visited to confirm whether a booking can be completed online, or only an enquiry sent. This is a hand-audited convenience sample weighted toward Malaysia's tourism hubs; the website figure has stayed within the 72–74% band across sampling rounds, and the 8% bookable figure is a live check of every website-owning operator, not an estimate.
