For Boat, Fishing & Charter Operators

We know how charter bookings actually work

The coordination behind every departure — and why generic tools miss it.

Most charter operators aren’t short on demand — they’re short on certainty. A boat takes a fixed number, a departure is set by the tide or the booking sheet, and a no-show on a six-person reef charter you’ve fuelled and crewed is money straight off the day. The booking is easy. Knowing exactly who’s coming, that they’ve paid their deposit, and that the trip will run — that’s the hard part.

And it’s an industry-wide gap. Kong’s operator research found that 78% of Malaysian boat, island-hopping and cruise operators have no website of their own (n=49) — the category charters fall in.

Generic booking software is built for appointments — one customer, one slot. A charter is the opposite: a shared departure with a hard capacity limit, prepaid or deposit-secured so you’re not left out of pocket when the weather turns, with guest details you need before they step on board. Kong is the charter booking software built around how departure-based boat and fishing operations actually run.

The Fit

What charter operators need from booking software

What a departure-based boat, fishing or cruise operation needs from its booking system — on the web or the Kong app.

Capacity per departure, never oversold

Set how many seats each departure takes and Kong stops booking the moment it’s full — no overbooked boat, no turning guests away at the marina.

Pricing that adjusts to group size

Guests pick how many seats and the price updates — a couple, a family or a full boat charter — so you stop quoting per-head totals by hand.

Deposits, so a no-show doesn’t cost you the day

Kong takes a deposit or full payment when the guest books, via Stripe — so a fuelled, crewed departure isn’t left out of pocket by a last-minute cancellation.

Block out a day for weather or maintenance

Close a departure or a whole day in one click when the forecast turns or the boat’s in for a service — your booking pages stop taking bookings for it instantly.

Guest details captured up front

Names, numbers, party size and any notes come in with the booking, so you’ve got your manifest before anyone steps on board.

Confirmations and reminders, automatic

A confirmation email at booking and a reminder before departure — fewer no-shows for a trip you’ve already crewed and provisioned.

Calendar & Manifest

Your week at a glance

Every booking lands on the right departure, so each day’s manifest and guest list build themselves. Block out a charter for a private group, or close a day for weather or maintenance in one click — your booking pages stop taking bookings for it instantly.
