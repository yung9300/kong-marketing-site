Comparisons

Compare Kong to other booking platforms

Honest, calibrated comparisons — written by Blake Ng, co-founder of Kong, who spent six years at Rezdy, Checkfront, and Regiondo. Each one is upfront about where the competitor genuinely wins, not just where Kong does.

Kong vs Bokun

Tripadvisor-owned

Deep Viator integration with a 0% Viator fee. Cheaper if Viator drives most of your bookings — but it's owned by the OTA you may be trying to diversify away from.

Read comparison

Kong vs Rezdy

Sydney-based

Powerful and highly configurable, with mature OTA connections — but expensive, complex to set up, and built for large established operators.

Read comparison

Kong vs Checkfront

Post-merger entity

Stable and broad — handles rentals and accommodation well. Same combined company as Rezdy post-merger, with a dated guest-facing booking form.

Read comparison

Kong vs FareHarbor

6% guest fee

The most feature-complete platform here, with excellent support — but up to a 6% fee added at checkout, which converts poorly in Southeast Asia.

Read comparison

Kong vs Bookeo

Flat-fee

A predictable flat monthly bill with no booking commissions — but no real OTA distribution, and the tiered caps scale poorly as you grow.

Read comparison

Kong vs Amelia

WordPress plugin

A polished WordPress booking plugin built for appointment businesses — fine if you live inside WordPress, but not built for multi-channel tour operators.
