TOBTAB Licence Explained: Malaysia's Tour Operating Licence (2026)

Blake Ng
Co-founder, Kong June 2026 6 min read

Last updated: June 2026

If you're getting licensed as a tour operator in Malaysia, the first term you'll hit is TOBTAB — the official name of the licence you need — closely followed by TTMC, the course you have to pass to get it. They're easy to confuse: one is the licence, the other is a course. Here's what the TOBTAB licence is, how the TTMC course fits in, and who needs what.

What is TOBTAB?

TOBTAB stands for Tour Operating Business and Travel Agency Business — it's the name of the official licence Malaysian tour and travel companies need to operate legally, issued by the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture (MOTAC). When someone says they need "the MOTAC licence" to run tours, the TOBTAB licence is what they mean.

The licence covers tour operating and travel agency activity, and it comes in different scopes — inbound (tours within Malaysia), outbound (overseas travel), and ticketing — depending on what your business does. To hold a TOBTAB licence you'll need a registered Sdn Bhd company, commercial premises, the TTMC course (below), membership of a recognised tourism association, and an application through MOTAC's TOURLIST portal.

What is the TTMC course?

The TTMC — the Travel and Tours Management Course — is a mandatory, MOTAC-accredited training course that at least one key person in your company must complete and pass before you can be granted a new TOBTAB licence. It ends with an assessment, so it's a genuine course with a pass requirement, not just a registration step.

The TTMC exists so that licensed operators have at least one person trained in how the industry and its regulations actually work. You complete it as part of getting your TOBTAB licence, typically through a MOTAC-accredited training provider.

TOBTAB vs TTMC vs TTEC vs MOTAC — untangling the acronyms

Once you're licensed, remember the obligations don't stop there — if you provide accommodation as part of your packages, check our guide to the tourism tax in Malaysia.

Here's the one-paragraph version that clears up the confusion. MOTAC is the ministry (the regulator). TOBTAB is the licence MOTAC issues. TTMC is the course you must pass to get that licence for the first time. And TTEC — the Travel and Tours Enhancement Course — is the equivalent course for when you renew an existing licence rather than apply for a new one. So: MOTAC regulates, TOBTAB is the licence, TTMC gets you in, TTEC keeps you current.

Who needs to complete the TTMC?

At least one key company representative — usually a director, or your designated manager — must complete the TTMC for your company to qualify for a new licence. It does not have to be every employee; it's a per-company requirement met by a key person.

Separately from the course, your designated manager must also meet a qualification bar: either a tourism-related diploma or degree, or a minimum of three years' experience in the tourism industry. The TTMC and the manager-qualification requirement are two different things, and you'll generally need to satisfy both.

How TOBTAB and TTMC fit into getting licensed

The TTMC is one step inside the larger process of getting your TOBTAB licence. The full path runs: register a Sdn Bhd company with SSM, secure commercial premises, complete the TTMC course, join a MOTAC-recognised tourism association, and submit your application through MOTAC's TOURLIST portal with the application fee.

We've written the complete step-by-step version — including capital requirements, premises rules, timelines and fees — in our full MOTAC licence guide. This article is just the part that explains the two acronyms people get stuck on.

Frequently asked questions

What does TOBTAB stand for?
TOBTAB stands for Tour Operating Business and Travel Agency Business. It's the name of the licence the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture (MOTAC) issues to tour and travel businesses in Malaysia.
What is a TOBTAB licence?
A TOBTAB licence is the official MOTAC licence that lets a Malaysian company legally operate as a tour operator or travel agency. It's what people usually mean by "the MOTAC licence." To get one you need a registered Sdn Bhd, commercial premises, the TTMC course, tourism-association membership, and an application through MOTAC's TOURLIST portal.
How do I get a TOBTAB licence from MOTAC?
You apply through MOTAC's TOURLIST portal after putting the pieces in place: register a Sdn Bhd company, secure commercial premises (which MOTAC inspects), complete the TTMC course, and join a recognised tourism association. The full step-by-step process, including capital requirements and fees, is in our MOTAC licence guide.
What is the TTMC course?
The TTMC (Travel and Tours Management Course) is a mandatory, MOTAC-accredited course that at least one key person in your company must complete and pass to be granted a new TOBTAB licence. It ends with an assessment.
Is the TTMC the same as TOBTAB?
No. TOBTAB is the licence; the TTMC is the course you must pass to get the licence. You complete the TTMC as one step in applying for your TOBTAB licence.
Who needs to take the TTMC?
At least one key company representative — typically a director or the designated manager — must complete the TTMC. It's a per-company requirement, not something every employee needs to do individually.
Is the TTMC mandatory?
Yes. For new TOBTAB (tour operating) licence applications, completing the TTMC is a mandatory requirement set by MOTAC.
What's the difference between TTMC and TTEC?
The TTMC (Travel and Tours Management Course) is for new licence applications. The TTEC (Travel and Tours Enhancement Course) is the equivalent course for renewing an existing licence. Both are MOTAC-accredited.
